Upanishad Gyanamrit

✦
The Upanishads

The Upanishads are the Vedanta — the very end and essence of the Vedas. These 108 sacred texts contain the direct inquiry into the nature of Brahman, Atman and the ultimate reality. They are the highest philosophical and spiritual knowledge given to humanity.

☸
Gyan Through Bhakti

Jagadguru Shri Kripalu Ji Maharaj revealed that true Upanishadic wisdom is not merely intellectual — it must be lived through devotion. Pujaniya Raseshwari Devi Ji carries this living tradition, making the mahavakyas of the Upanishads a direct experience for every attendee.

☽
Transformation Through Discourse

Attending Upanishad Gyanamrit is a rare opportunity to receive the highest Vedic knowledge directly from an illumined teacher. These discourses purify the intellect, remove the veil of ignorance and plant the seed of true self-knowledge in the heart of every seeker.

Frequently Asked Questions

"श्रद्धावान् लभते ज्ञानम्" — The faithful one attains knowledge · Bhagavad Gita 4.39

Who is Pujaniya Raseshwari Devi Ji? ＋
Pujaniya Raseshwari Devi Ji is the founder of Braj Gopika Seva Mission (BGSM) and a preacher of the teachings of Jagadguru Shri Kripalu Ji Maharaj. She has dedicated her life to the spiritual upliftment of seekers through discourses, satsangs and service. Known equally as A Disciple, A Leader, A Mother and A Humanitarian, she has touched millions of lives across India and the world.
What is Upanishad Gyanamrit? ＋
Upanishad Gyanamrit is a unique philosophical discourse series by Pujaniya Raseshwari Devi Ji on the ancient Upanishads — the Vedanta, the highest wisdom of the Vedas. These discourses illuminate timeless spiritual truths in a way that is accessible, devotional and deeply transformative for seekers of all backgrounds.
Where and when is the Janjgir programme? ＋
The programme will be held from 25th September to 8th October, 2026, every evening from 6:30 PM to 8:30 PM at High School Ground, Janjgir, Chhattisgarh. Entry is completely free and all are welcome.
Do I need prior knowledge of the Upanishads to attend? ＋
Not at all. Pujaniya Raseshwari Devi Ji makes the deepest Vedantic wisdom simple, relatable and alive for everyone — whether you are a first-time seeker or a long-time devotee. The discourses are conducted in a warm, devotional setting that welcomes all ages and backgrounds.
What is BGSM — Braj Gopika Seva Mission? ＋
Braj Gopika Seva Mission (BGSM) is the organisation founded by Pujaniya Raseshwari Devi Ji, rooted in the teachings of Jagadguru Shri Kripalu Ji Maharaj. With 130+ nodal centers and a global reach of 10 million+ lives, BGSM works for the spiritual, humanitarian and holistic wellbeing of society through satsang, seva and sadhana.
